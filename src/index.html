      What's the plan?

      REWARDS

      Spend your XP

      Every XP you earn does two things. It fills your level bar, which never goes down, and it lands in your wallet, which you can spend here.

      1,240XP
      Wallet balance. Spending XP never lowers your level. The partner offers below are sample offers at real Bologna venues.

      Level unlocks

      Free. They open up as your level goes up.

      Venue perks

      Real coffee, gelato and gym passes. Venues pay to list them.

      My vouchers

      Show the code at the counter. Valid for 7 days.

      Look & titles

      Cosmetic only. Buy once, then equip.

      Boosts

      Small advantages on the board that run out after a while.

      Crew rewards

      Earned together, so people keep coming back as a group.

      Full crew bonus

      When everyone in the crew checks in, each person gets 20% extra XP.

      Crew-only offers

      Some venue perks need a crew, like a free tagliere for 4 people who check in together.

      District crown

      The crew with the most missions in a neighbourhood each month gets its colour on that district and a crown badge.

      Crew XP pot

      Named crews share a pot of XP they can spend together on a group perk.

      NEVER FOR SALE

      Women-only missions, ID verification, SOS, live sharing with a contact, reporting and blocking are free for everyone at every level. Nobody can buy their way past a host's approval either.
